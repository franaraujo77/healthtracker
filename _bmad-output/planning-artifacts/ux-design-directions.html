Design Direction Showcase

Health Tracker — UX Directions

Six distinct home-screen concepts for a patient-owned longitudinal health record. All directions share the same design system. Click any direction to mark it as selected.

Evaluation Criteria
Emotional Resonance
Does the first screen make Maria feel seen, not diagnosed? Does it communicate her own trajectory rather than population norms?
Longitudinal Priority
Does the direction foreground change over time rather than the latest snapshot? Does it reward returning users with more context?
Cognitive Load
Can Maria understand her health situation within 5 seconds? Are numbers always contextualised — never raw values alone?
Archetype Fit
Does it serve both The Organiser (PDF-upload, systematic) and The WhatsApp-native (camera, casual)? Neither should feel excluded.
Sharing Ceremony Fit
How naturally does the sharing flow emerge from this home screen? Does the direction support the Doctor Conversation Starter pattern?
Delight & Craft
Is there a moment of warmth or surprise? Does the direction go beyond utility into something Maria would describe as "beautiful" or "mine"?
